# Vimivo User Research Policy & Ethical Consent Framework

## 1. Principles of Ethical User Research

Vimivo does not conduct surreptitious user tracking, telemetry collection, or covert behavioral surveillance. When conducting usability testing, beta evaluations, or user interviews:

1. **Explicit Informed Consent**: Participants must be explicitly informed of what data is collected, how it is used, and their right to withdraw at any time.
2. **Zero In-App Telemetry**: No software build of Vimivo will ever contain hidden tracking code to observe user behavior without consent.
3. **No Private Media Ingestion**: Usability tests use standardized, open-source media files (e.g. Creative Commons licensed films or public domain classical music). Users are never asked to expose personal media libraries or private browsing habits.
4. **No Fabricated Personas or Testimonials**: We never invent user interviews, survey responses, or community feedback to justify product decisions. All research artifacts reflect verified, authentic participant interactions.

---

## 2. Opt-In Beta Research Protocol

1. **Scope**: Limited to volunteers running explicitly marked beta builds.
2. **Delivery Mechanism**: Feedback forms and diagnostic reports are submitted manually via GitHub Issues or email templates provided in `docs/BETA_TESTER_GUIDE.md`.
3. **Data Anonymization**: All feedback is anonymized before sharing with the broader development team. Personal identifiers (names, email addresses, usernames) are scrubbed.
4. **Right to Erasure**: Any participant may request deletion of their feedback drafts or research notes from our tracking registers upon request.
