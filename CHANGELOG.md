# Changelog

## 1.0.0 - Unreleased

- Initial Android release candidate.
- Local audio and video library with MediaStore and system-picker access.
- Unified Media3 and LibVLC playback architecture with one background media session.
- Music queues, playlists, favorites, history, bookmarks, search, and resume positions.
- Fullscreen video, gestures, PiP, subtitles, multiple audio tracks, and playback controls.
- Authorized direct URL, HLS, DASH, and internet-radio playback.
- Appearance, playback, accessibility, privacy, and media-library settings.
- Versioned local backup and restore without media-file duplication.
